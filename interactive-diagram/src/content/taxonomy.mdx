## Taxonomic Classification

The processing workflow classifies bins, contigs, and recovered rRNA sequences.

### Bin Taxonomy

`Taxonomy_bins` runs GTDB-Tk `classify_wf` over `results/bins/*.fa`. The wrapper must bind the GTDB database path and the Snakefile sets `GTDBTK_DATA_PATH`.

### Contig Taxonomy

`Taxonomy_contigs` runs Metabuli on the final assembly FASTA and keeps a simplified table of contig IDs and lineage assignments.

### 16S rRNA Taxonomy

`Taxonomy_rrna` uses Barrnap to extract rRNA sequences, keeps the configured SSU type, links each rRNA sequence back to its bin when possible, and classifies sequences with Usearch against the rRNA database.

Exact defaults:

- `ssu = 16S`
- `min_len_ssu = 500`
- `min_id_ssu = 0.75`

The aggregate rule merges GTDB and rRNA-based taxonomy for bins and Metabuli/rRNA taxonomy for contigs.
