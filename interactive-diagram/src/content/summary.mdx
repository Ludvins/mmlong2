## Lite Summary Outputs

The production workflow ends by calculating bin coverage, relative abundance, assembly statistics, and resource usage.

### Coverage and Abundance

`Summary_coverage` uses CoverM over accepted bin FASTAs and the primary-read BAMs:

- `cov_method = mean`
- relative abundance is calculated as a separate CoverM metric

### Bin Statistics

`Summary_stats` runs QUAST over final bins and creates a contig-to-bin map. The `Finalise` rule merges:

- CheckM
- CheckM2
- QUAST
- CoverM coverage
- CoverM relative abundance
- workflow metadata

The key production output is `results/{sample}_bins.tsv`, which is later enriched by the processing workflow.
