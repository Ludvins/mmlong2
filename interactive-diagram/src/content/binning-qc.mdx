## Binning QC and Final MAG Selection

mmlong2 uses two quality gates after candidate binning: a per-round CheckM2-style filter and a final CheckM lineage filter. This means binner output is never accepted solely because a method produced it.

### Round-Specific CheckM2 Filter

`get_qc_in()` chooses which candidate source is evaluated:

| mode or round | candidate source |
| --- | --- |
| `fast` | SemiBin2 `bins_info.tsv` and `output_bins` |
| extended round 2 | COMEBin `comebin_res.tsv` and `comebin_res_bins` |
| extended round 4 | MetaBAT2 `bins_metabat2.MemberMatrix.txt` and FASTA bins |
| default / other rounds | Binette `final_bins_quality_reports.tsv` and `final_bins` |

The diagram shows this with a separate source-selector node because it is not a linear dependency:

<div className="formula-card">

$$
\operatorname{qc\_source}(r,\text{binmode}) =
\begin{cases}
\text{SemiBin2}, & \text{binmode = fast}\\
\text{COMEBin}, & \text{binmode = extended and } r=2\\
\text{MetaBAT2}, & \text{binmode = extended and } r=4\\
\text{Binette}, & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

</div>

The per-round keep rule is:

<div className="formula-card">

$$
\operatorname{keep}_{round}(b)
=
\mathbb{1}
\left[
C_{CheckM2}(b)\ge C_{min}
\right]
\cdot
\mathbb{1}
\left[
K_{CheckM2}(b)\le K_{max}
\right]
$$

</div>

where `C` is completeness and `K` is contamination.

### Exact mmlong2 Thresholds

<div className="method-grid">
  <div className="method-card">
    <strong>Extended round 1</strong>
    Completeness at least <code>90</code>; contamination at most <code>5</code>.
  </div>
  <div className="method-card">
    <strong>Default, fast, and other extended rounds</strong>
    Completeness at least <code>50</code>; contamination at most <code>10</code>.
  </div>
</div>

These values come from `get_compl()` and `get_cont()`:

<div className="formula-card">

$$
C_{min} =
\begin{cases}
90, & \text{round 1 and binmode = extended}\\
50, & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

$$
K_{max} =
\begin{cases}
5, & \text{round 1 and binmode = extended}\\
10, & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

</div>

### Aggregation Before Final CheckM

`Binning_aggregate` copies kept bins from singleton recovery and all binning rounds into `tmp/binning/bins_innit`, then concatenates matching CheckM2 rows into `tmp/binning/checkm2.tsv`.

This is the join point that makes the graph non-linear:

<div className="formula-card">

$$
\text{MAG candidates}
=
\text{kept singleton bins}
\cup
\bigcup_{r \in \text{active rounds}}
\text{kept round-}r\text{ bins}
$$

</div>

Singletons therefore bypass MetaBAT2/VAMB/SemiBin2/COMEBin/Binette, but they do not bypass the final CheckM gate.

### Final CheckM Gate

`Binning_qc2` runs:

```bash
checkm lineage_wf -x fa -t {threads} \
  -f tmp/binning/checkm1.tsv \
  --pplacer_threads $(({threads} / 2)) \
  --reduced_tree --tab_table \
  tmp/binning/bins_innit tmp/binning/checkm1
```

The final keep expression is exactly the AWK filter in `src/mmlong2-lite.smk`:

<div className="formula-card">

$$
C_{CheckM}(b) \ge 50
\quad\land\quad
K_{CheckM}(b) \le 10
\quad\land\quad
C_{CheckM}(b) - 5K_{CheckM}(b) > 30
$$

</div>

Bins passing that final expression are copied to `results/bins`.
