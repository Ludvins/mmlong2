### Method Summary

Binette is the ensemble selector used by mmlong2 in default binning rounds. It consumes bin directories from VAMB, MetaBAT2, and SemiBin2, predicts bin quality with CheckM2, derives/refines candidate bins, and writes the final per-round Binette bin set.

This is a different use of "ensemble" from SemiBin2's long-read DBSCAN ensemble. Binette ensembles **different binner outputs**; SemiBin2 ensembles **different DBSCAN epsilon clusterings inside one tool**.

### Candidate Inputs

mmlong2's `Binning_binette` rule requires all three upstream binners to finish first:

- VAMB: `round_{n}/vamb/vae_clusters_unsplit.tsv` and `round_{n}/vamb/bins`
- MetaBAT2: `round_{n}/metabat2/bins_metabat2.MemberMatrix.txt` and FASTA bins
- SemiBin2: `round_{n}/semibin/bins_info.tsv` and `round_{n}/semibin/output_bins`
- active contigs: `round_{n}/contigs.fasta`

The actual command is:

```bash
binette -d $dir/vamb/bins $dir/metabat2 $dir/semibin/output_bins \
  -c round_{n}/contigs.fasta \
  -o $dir/binette \
  -w 5 \
  -m {50_or_90} \
  -t {threads}
```

### Quality Score

Binette reports CheckM2-derived completeness and contamination, then computes a configurable score. mmlong2 sets the contamination weight to `5`.

<div className="formula-card">

$$
\operatorname{score}(b)
=
\operatorname{completeness}(b)
-
\operatorname{contamination}(b)\cdot w
$$

$$
w_{\mathrm{mmlong2}} = 5
$$

</div>

This is the exact score definition documented by Binette. It is also aligned with mmlong2's later final CheckM score gate.

### Outputs Used by mmlong2

Binette writes:

- `final_bins_quality_reports.tsv`
- `final_bins/`
- `final_contig_to_bin.tsv`
- `input_bins_quality_reports/`
- `temporary_files/`

The next rule, `Binning_qc`, uses `final_bins_quality_reports.tsv` specially: because Binette has already run CheckM2-like quality reporting, mmlong2 cuts columns `1,4,5` from that report instead of rerunning CheckM2 for that branch.

### Round Thresholds

The `-m` value passed to Binette comes from:

<div className="formula-card">

$$
m_{\mathrm{Binette}} =
\begin{cases}
90, & \text{round 1 and binmode = extended}\\
50, & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

</div>

After Binette, the selected bins still have to pass mmlong2's CheckM2 round thresholds and final CheckM thresholds before they reach `results/bins`.
