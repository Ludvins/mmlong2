## Assembly Curation

The curation block screens the selected assembly for evidence of misassembly. It maps reads back to contigs, detects suspicious low-coverage or clipping patterns, aggregates those intervals, and writes a curated FASTA plus updated assembly info.

### Signal Model

mmlong2 does not train a statistical model here. It applies rule-based thresholds to read-mapping evidence. Conceptually, each candidate interval is judged from local support:

$$
\text{keep}(r) =
\mathbb{1}[\text{coverage}(r) \ge c_{\min}]
\cdot
\mathbb{1}[\text{clip support}(r) < \rho]
$$

where the concrete thresholds are the mmlong2 config values below.

### Exact mmlong2 Defaults

- `curation_status = TRUE`
- `min_dist = 500`
- `min_len_zerocov = 100`
- `clip_ratio = 0.9`
- `min_cov_clip = 3`
- `min_cov_clip_all = 3`

The important implementation detail is that curation changes both sequence and metadata. Later circular-contig extraction and binning rely on the curated assembly info when curation is enabled.
