## Final Dataframes

The final processing rule merges the production and processing outputs into analysis-ready tables.

### Main Outputs

- `results/{sample}_general.tsv`
- `results/{sample}_contigs.tsv`
- `results/{sample}_bins.tsv`
- `results/{sample}_usage.tsv`
- `results/dependencies.csv`
- `results/databases.csv`
- `results/bins/*.fa`
- `results/bakta/`

### Bin Quality Status

mmlong2 assigns `bin_status` using CheckM2 completeness/contamination plus annotation completeness signals. A high-quality bin needs high completeness, low contamination, sufficient tRNA uniqueness, and rRNA evidence. Medium and low quality are then assigned by relaxed completeness and contamination thresholds; otherwise the bin is marked contaminated.

The final tables include workflow metadata:

- `wf_name`
- `wf_read_mode`
- `wf_binning_mode`
- `wf_v`
- `wf_date`
