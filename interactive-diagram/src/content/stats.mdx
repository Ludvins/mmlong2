## Read, Assembly, and Mapping Statistics

The processing stats stage calculates summary values that are independent of the binning algorithm internals.

### Sequence Stats

`Stats_seq` uses Nanoq for reads and contigs. `Stats_aggregate` keeps read counts, yield, N50, length extrema, mean/median lengths, and mean/median quality where available.

### Mapping Stats

`Stats_map` uses Cramino over the primary-read alignment. mmlong2 records:

- mean coverage
- mapped yield in Gbp
- median read identity
- mean read identity

### Final General Table

The processing `Finalise` rule computes derived values such as:

$$
\operatorname{yield\_assembled}
=
100 \cdot
\frac{\operatorname{map\_yield\_gbp} \cdot 10^9}{\operatorname{reads\_yield\_bp}}
$$

and uses bin relative abundance to estimate binned yield.
