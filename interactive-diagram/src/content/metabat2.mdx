### Method Summary

MetaBAT2 is the graph-based, non-neural binner in the mmlong2 ensemble. It receives the round FASTA plus the full coverage table `cov.tsv`, writes FASTA bins, and writes `bins_metabat2.MemberMatrix.txt` for downstream Binette/QC logic.

### Features

MetaBAT2 combines tetranucleotide-frequency similarity and abundance similarity. mmlong2's `Coverage_map` creates per-read depth files, and `Coverage_aggregate` pastes them into the `cov.tsv` table consumed here.

\left[
\operatorname{TNF}_i,\,
\mathbf{a}_i
\right]
$$

$$
\mathbf{a}_i =
\left[
d_{i,1}, d_{i,2}, \ldots, d_{i,m}

### Adaptive Composite Score

The MetaBAT2 paper states that raw TNF and abundance scores are normalized, then combined. With fewer than three effective abundance samples, the composite score is:

<div className="formula-card">

$$
w =
\frac{n_{ABD}}{n_{ABD}+1}
$$

$$
S =
\operatorname{TNF}^{1-w}
\operatorname{ABD}^{w}
$$

</div>

where `n_ABD` is the number of effective abundance samples. The more effective samples there are, the more weight the abundance score receives.

When at least three samples are available, MetaBAT2 also calculates an abundance-correlation score `COR` and uses:

<div className="formula-card">

$$
S =
\sqrt{
\operatorname{TNF}^{1-w}
\operatorname{ABD}^{w}
\operatorname{COR}
}
$$

</div>

`S` becomes an edge weight in graph-based clustering.

### Graph Partitioning

MetaBAT2 first builds a graph with contigs as nodes using strong TNF edges. It then iteratively incorporates high-scoring `S` edges and partitions the graph with a modified label-propagation algorithm. The published modifications are important for interpreting reproducibility:

- search order is driven by edge strength, so partitioning is deterministic rather than random;
- previous-round partition labels seed the next round;
- Fisher's method evaluates likely contig membership across neighborhoods using `1 - S` as p-values.

### Role in mmlong2

In default binmode, MetaBAT2 is not the final arbiter. Its FASTA bins and membership matrix are one input to Binette. In extended mode, round 4 can use MetaBAT2 output directly for the CheckM2 round filter through `get_qc_in()` and `get_qc_dir()`.
