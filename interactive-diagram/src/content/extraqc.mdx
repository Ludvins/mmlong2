## Extra QC

Extra QC adds two signals that are not part of the core MAG recovery loop: nucleotide microdiversity and chimerism/contamination evidence.

### Variant-Based Microdiversity

mmlong2 prepares contig FASTA slices, extracts relevant alignments from the primary-read BAM, and calls variants with Longshot.

Exact defaults:

- `min_cov = 6`
- `min_mapq = 18`
- `min_frac = 0.1`
- `min_count = 3`

The final tables report variant counts and variant percentage at contig and bin levels:

$$
\operatorname{var\_perc}_{bin}
=
100 \cdot \frac{\operatorname{var\_n}_{bin}}{\operatorname{genome\_size}_{bin}}
$$

### GUNC

`ExtraQC_contamination` runs GUNC over accepted bins and records clade separation score, reference representation score, and pass/fail status.
