### Method Summary

COMEBin appears in mmlong2's extended binning branch. mmlong2 prepares round-specific contigs and BAM subsets, then runs `run_comebin.sh`. This node represents COMEBin's neural representation learning, data augmentation, and NT-Xent loss. The downstream `COMEBin Leiden clustering` node shows how those embeddings become candidate bins.

### Views and Feature Vectors

COMEBin constructs six views for each original contig: one original view plus five augmented views from randomly extracted continuous fragments. It builds both composition and coverage features.

<div className="formula-card">

$$
{F}_{i}=({f}_{i,1},\ldots,{f}_{i,136})
$$

$$
{q}_{i,j}=
\frac{{f}_{i,j}^{\prime}}
{\sum_{k=1}^{136}{f}_{i,k}^{\prime}},
\quad
{f}_{i,j}^{\prime}={f}_{i,j}+1
$$

$$
\mathbf{x}_{i}^{(com)}
=
[{q}_{i,1},\ldots,{q}_{i,136}]
$$

</div>

The COMEBin paper uses tetramer features with reverse complements treated as equivalent, giving 136 composition dimensions.

For coverage, COMEBin concatenates mean and standard-deviation coverage vectors over `M` samples:

<div className="formula-card">

$$
\mathbf{C}_{i}
=
\left(
\mathbf{C}_{i}^{(mean)},\,
\mathbf{C}_{i}^{(std)}
\right)
$$

$$
x_{i,j}^{(cov)}
=
\frac{c_{i,m}^{\prime}}
{\max_{k=1}^{N} c_{k,m}^{\prime}},
\quad
c_{i,m}^{\prime}=c_{i,m}+10^{-5}
$$

</div>

`N` is the number of contigs. In mmlong2, COMEBin sees the BAMs in `round_{n}/mapping`, after `Comebin_prep_*` has subset alignments to active contigs for that round.

### Networks

COMEBin uses two feed-forward modules:

<div className="method-grid">
  <div className="method-card">
    <strong>Coverage network</strong>
    Takes coverage features and maps variable sample coverage into embedded coverage representations.
  </div>
  <div className="method-card">
    <strong>Combine network</strong>
    Normalizes coverage-network outputs, concatenates them with k-mer features, and outputs heterogeneous contig embeddings.
  </div>
</div>

### Multi-View NT-Xent Objective

For two views, COMEBin defines an NT-Xent pair loss:

<div className="formula-card">

$$
\ell(\mathbf{z}_{i,k},\mathbf{z}_{i,k'})
=
-\log
\frac{
\exp(\cos(\mathbf{z}_{i,k},\mathbf{z}_{i,k'})/\tau)
}{
\sum_{s=1}^{N_{bs}}
\left[
\mathbb{1}_{[s\ne i]}
\exp(\cos(\mathbf{z}_{i,k},\mathbf{z}_{s,k})/\tau)
+
\exp(\cos(\mathbf{z}_{i,k},\mathbf{z}_{s,k'})/\tau)
\right]
}
$$

$$
\cos(\mathbf{a},\mathbf{b})
=
\frac{\mathbf{a}^{T}\mathbf{b}}
{|\mathbf{a}|\,|\mathbf{b}|}
$$

</div>

The full six-view loss used by COMEBin is:

<div className="formula-card">

$$
\mathcal{L}
=
-\frac{1}{N_{bs}V(V-1)}
\sum_{i=1}^{N_{bs}}
\sum_{v=1}^{V}
\sum_{\substack{v_1=1\\v_1\ne v}}^{V}
\log
\frac{
\exp(\cos(\mathbf{z}_{i,v},\mathbf{z}_{i,v_1})/\tau)
}{
\exp(\cos(\mathbf{z}_{i,v},\mathbf{z}_{i,v_1})/\tau)
+
\sum_{j=1}^{N_{bs}}
\sum_{v_2=1}^{V}
\mathbb{1}_{[j\ne i]}
\exp(\cos(\mathbf{z}_{i,v},\mathbf{z}_{j,v_2})/\tau)
}
$$

</div>

The paper uses `V = 6`, `batch size = 1024`, 200 training epochs with early stopping, and `tau = 0.07` for assemblies with N50 above 10,000 bp, otherwise `tau = 0.15`. mmlong2 mirrors the batch-size ceiling by passing `-b min(number_of_contigs, 1024)`.

### Leiden Clustering Handoff

After training, the `COMEBin Leiden clustering` node builds a nearest-neighbor graph from contig embeddings and converts distances to similarities:

<div className="formula-card">

$$
S_{ij}
=
\exp
\left(
\frac{
-\left\|\mathbf{x}_i-\mathbf{x}_j\right\|^2
}{\sigma}
\right)
$$

</div>

COMEBin sweeps sigma values `{0.05, 0.1, 0.15, 0.2, 0.3}`, Leiden resolutions `{1, 5, 10, 30, 50, 70, 90, 110}`, and edge-retention ratios `{50%, 80%, 100%}`. It estimates bin quality for each result and chooses the result with the best aggregate quality signal.

### Role in mmlong2

In mmlong2, COMEBin is a round-specific candidate binner in extended mode. Its output flows into CheckM2 round filtering rather than Binette in round 2, according to `get_qc_in()` and `get_qc_dir()`.
