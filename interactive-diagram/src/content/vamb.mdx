### Method Summary

VAMB is the variational autoencoder branch of mmlong2's default binning ensemble. mmlong2 does not train or tune VAMB directly; it calls `vamb bin default` and passes the round FASTA, the reduced abundance table, seed, minimum contig length, minimum bin size, and threads. This node represents the VAE/latent representation part; the downstream `VAMB latent clustering` node shows how those latent vectors become candidate bins.

### Encoder Inputs

VAMB's documented input types are k-mer composition and abundance. In mmlong2, the abundance side comes from `Coverage_aggregate`, specifically `cov_all_sub.tsv`.

<div className="formula-card">

$$
\mathbf{x}_i =
\left[
\mathbf{k}_i,\,
\mathbf{a}_i
\right]
$$

$$
\mathbf{a}_i =
\left[
d_{i,1}, d_{i,2}, \ldots, d_{i,m}
\right]
$$

</div>

Here, `i` indexes a contig, `k_i` is its composition feature vector, and `a_i` is its abundance vector over the primary and differential-coverage read sets. mmlong2 prepares that table; VAMB decides how to normalize and feed it into its model.

### VAE Encoder and Latent Representation

VAMB encodes each contig into a latent representation `z`. The standard VAE form is:

<div className="formula-card">

$$
q_\phi(\mathbf{z}_i \mid \mathbf{x}_i)
=
\mathcal{N}
\left(
\boldsymbol{\mu}_\phi(\mathbf{x}_i),
\operatorname{diag}(\boldsymbol{\sigma}^2_\phi(\mathbf{x}_i))
\right)
$$

$$
\mathbf{z}_i =
\boldsymbol{\mu}_\phi(\mathbf{x}_i)
+
\boldsymbol{\sigma}_\phi(\mathbf{x}_i) \odot \boldsymbol{\epsilon},
\quad
\boldsymbol{\epsilon} \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I)
$$

</div>

`mu_phi` and `sigma_phi` are produced by the encoder. The reparameterization line is the usual VAE trick that lets gradients pass through stochastic sampling.

### Loss Terms

The diagram shows the VAE objective at the level needed to reason about mmlong2 outputs. The exact coefficients are upstream VAMB defaults for the installed VAMB package.

<div className="formula-card">

$$
\mathcal{L}_i =
\lambda_k\,\mathcal{L}_k(\mathbf{k}_i,\hat{\mathbf{k}}_i)
+
\lambda_a\,\mathcal{L}_a(\mathbf{a}_i,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_i)
+
\lambda_{KL}\,
D_{KL}
\left(
q_\phi(\mathbf{z}_i\mid\mathbf{x}_i)
\parallel
\mathcal{N}(0,I)
\right)
$$

$$
D_{KL}
\left(
\mathcal{N}(\boldsymbol{\mu},\operatorname{diag}(\boldsymbol{\sigma}^2))
\parallel
\mathcal{N}(0,I)
\right)
=
\frac{1}{2}
\sum_\ell
\left(
\mu_\ell^2+\sigma_\ell^2-\log\sigma_\ell^2-1
\right)
$$

</div>

The first two terms reconstruct sequence composition and abundance. The KL term regularizes the latent space toward a unit Gaussian prior, making the embedding smoother for clustering.

### Clustering Handoff

The `VAMB latent clustering` node writes `vae_clusters_unsplit.tsv` and a `bins/` directory. In mmlong2 default mode, those bins are one proposal set for Binette beside MetaBAT2 and SemiBin2. They are not accepted directly unless downstream Binette, CheckM2, and final CheckM gates keep them.
